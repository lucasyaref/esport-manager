# M5.5 — Viewer v2: combat readability & juice

Status: **phases A–E done, runnable; F pending your 1x playtest** (that playtest is the gate).

From your 2026-07-25 playtest: remark 1 (champions stack on one point), remark 2 (fights are
unreadable — spells, auto-attacks, turret HP, ultimate impact aren't shown) and remark 4 (the CC
"control" wasn't visible). The sim already does all of this; the viewer just wasn't telling you.

## What to look at in-game

Run it and watch a fight at **1x**:

1. **Blows are traded.** Every auto-attack the sim fires now draws a beat — a projectile crossing
   to its target for a ranged character, a slash for a melee one, a spark where it lands.
2. **The catch reads as a catch.** When a CC lands, the victim wears a mark for exactly as long as
   the sim's lock lasts (icy ring + spurs for a slow, amber ring with stars orbiting the head for a
   stun/root/knockup) and a dashed tether ties it back to whoever cast it. Watch a jungle gank: the
   slow lands, the tether appears, the victim can't get home.
3. **Ultimates land.** One shape per effect family, bigger and louder than the same family from the
   basic-ability slot: shockwave at the ability's real radius for AoE, a beam for single-target
   (piercing for a snipe, a cross stamped on the victim for an execute), a bloom on everyone healed
   or shielded, an aura for a self-buff. The ability name now sits on a dark pill so it survives a
   busy fight.
4. **Sieges have a clock.** A turret shows a health bar once it has been chipped and flashes red
   under 35%; the nexus shows one when it starts taking hits. A dive under a low turret reads as
   the race it is.
5. **Characters, not dots.** One silhouette per role — broad hexagon top, arrowhead jungler, star
   mid, pentagon carry, octagon-and-cross support — team-tinted, turned toward its target or its
   path, with a facing pip. Bodies no longer stack: overlapping players are pushed apart for
   drawing, and handles in a crowd drop to their own line instead of printing over each other.
   Dead players fan out around the fountain instead of sitting on one grey dot.

Placeholder art contract is unchanged (CLAUDE.md): a character's `sprite` data field still
overrides the silhouette with a real texture, same tint and facing, no code change.

## The one real decision — separation is a *balance* question, not a rendering one

The backlog preferred a small **sim-side** body separation (bodies collide, which also spreads
combat naturally). I built it, measured it, and **did not ship it**. Same 200 sims, same seeds
(5000–5199), only difference being the separation pass:

| Metric | M5-D baseline | sim-side separation | target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azure Wolves (macro) win** | 36.5% | **30.5%** | ~43% |
| **Blue-side win** | 49.5% | **43.5%** | ~50% |
| **Gold-lead @15 → win** | 66.3% | **71.1%** | ~65% |
| Match length avg (min) | 26.9 | 26.6 | 25–35 |
| Kills / match | 22.0 | 23.6 | |
| First blood (min) | 4.8 | 4.1 | |
| Gank connect rate | 16% | 22% | |
| First tower is bot | 10% | 27% | |
| Lane swap rate (team-games) | 24% | 34% | |

Giving bodies volume changes fights: it costs the macro team ~6 points, undoes M5-D's blue-side
fix, and pushes snowball past its target. Interesting — the connect rate and bot-tower numbers go
*up*, which is the kind of thing M5-E/F wants — but it is a **design change with balance
consequences**, and it has no business riding in on a readability milestone.

So separation ships as a **playback layout pass** instead: overlapping bodies are relaxed apart
for drawing only, splitting each overlap evenly so the group's centre of mass is preserved — the
cluster still sits exactly where the fight is. The sim is untouched and **provably** so (below).

**Question for you (the only one I have):** should body collision become a real sim mechanic during
M5-E/F, tuned deliberately as part of the macro-split work? The numbers above suggest it pulls
several dials at once, so it is a real lever, not a cosmetic one.

  on the cast events.

## Sim-side additions (all playback-only)

Snapshot player rows are positional and append-only; playback reads combat state instead of
guessing it from the event stream:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9 | target row (who this player is attacking, −1 for nobody) |
| 10 | combat flags: in-combat / disengaging / stunned / slowed / recalling |
| 11 | tick of the last auto-attack fired |

Plus `towers` (only *chipped* structures) and `nexus` on each snapshot, a `cc_applied` event
(source, victim, kind, until, slot), and `pos` / `radius` / `targets` on `ultimate_cast` and
`basic_cast`.
