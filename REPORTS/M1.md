# M1 — Data model + content v1 — Report

## What changed
- **All game content now lives in [data/](../data/)** as designer-readable JSON: 15 characters ([characters.json](../data/characters.json)), 10 players ([players.json](../data/players.json)), 2 teams ([teams.json](../data/teams.json)), and the comp-logic rules ([comp_rules.json](../data/comp_rules.json)). Every value is yours to edit — see [data/README.md](../data/README.md) for what each field means.
- **Loader + validation** (`sim/data_loader.gd`): ~30 rules — schema, enums, cross-references (pools ↔ characters, rosters ↔ players), stat sanity ranges to catch typos. Wired into `tools/check.sh` and the in-game start screen. Verified it catches injected errors (wrong role name, absurd HP) with precise messages.
- **Report generator** (`tools/data_check.gd --report`) produced all tables below — future balance tweaks regenerate them in one command.

## Content design (my proposals — everything tunable in the files)
- **Characters**: each role has one early-game, one balanced/utility, one late-scaling option, so drafts always have a real tempo decision. Each has a unique ultimate and an `intent` one-liner in the file.
- **The two teams are deliberately asymmetric** to make pre-game decisions matter from the first match:
  - **Azure Wolves** — macro discipline (avg macro 83, compliance 87; mechanics only 71).
  - **Crimson Ravens** — raw mechanics (avg mechanics 84, laning 76; macro 68, compliance 62 — the superstar mid *Mirage* at 58 compliance will visibly ignore coach recos).
- Signature picks mirror team identity: Wolves main the scaling/protect characters, Ravens main the early/aggressive ones.

## What to look at in-game
Press Play: the start screen now shows "Game data: 15 characters, 10 players, 2 teams — VALID" above the determinism self-test. The real payoff of M1 is readable in the data files themselves.

## Open design questions
1. **Team asymmetry** (macro-discipline vs mechanics-aggression): the right first rivalry? Any renames wanted — team names, player handles, character names are all free to change in the JSON.
2. **Character fantasy check**: skim the 15 `intent` lines in characters.json — any kit that doesn't spark a draft-worthy fantasy? Cheap to swap now, expensive after M3 tuning.

(Stat *values* need no verdict yet — M3's 1000-match batch runs will judge balance with numbers.)
