# M6-T4 — camps and pits into their real pockets

**Status: built, guard rails clean, batch measured — did not do what it was diagnosed to do.** The
four camps that sat touching the dragon/baron pits now have a real gap between them. The two open
watch items T2 and T3 both pointed at this overlap for did not resolve — one held flat, the other
got worse. Design model: `REPORTS/M6-terrain-scoping.md` §2 finding 1, §3 "T4."

## 1. What it is

Four of the map's eight jungle camps (`b_camp_top`/`r_camp_top` near baron, `b_camp_bot`/
`r_camp_bot` near dragon) had their `data/terrain.txt` cell cluster directly touching their nearby
objective pit — zero-cell gap, confirmed by printing the grid before this phase started. That
specific "camp inside the pit" bug the original scoping report named (from the pre-T1 draft's raw
coordinates) had already been fixed during T1's 51 gauntlet iterations — but touching, not a real
separate pocket, is what was left.

- **`tools/terrain_paint.gd` gained a `--camps=D` mode** (`_move_camps`), following the same
  pattern as the existing `--pits=D` mode: find each camp within reach of its pit, translate its
  existing cell shape `D` world units directly away from the pit's centre (rounded to whole cells
  so `data/map.json`'s position and the moved terrain cells stay exactly aligned), and mirror the
  identical edit 180° onto its rotational partner automatically — same "fix one, the tool proves
  the other," not two independent hand edits that can drift apart.
- Chose **translation over redraw-from-scratch**: the first attempt rasterized a fresh 5-cell disc
  at the new position and corrupted a neighbouring camp's hand-authored shape in the process
  (`b_camp_top`'s new home was only 8.5 units from its own buff camp) — caught before writing,
  reverted, rebuilt as an exact cell-shift instead, which only ever touches a camp's own cells.
- **`D=6`** (world units): the smallest offset that cleared the neighbouring buff camp cleanly while
  giving a real gap, not a value derived from a target number — same "chosen by feel, tell the
  designer" honesty as T3's `brush_reveal_radius`.
- New positions: `b_camp_top` (23,53)→(17,51), `r_camp_top` (33,63)→(35,69), and their mirrors
  `r_camp_bot` (77,47)→(83,49), `b_camp_bot` (67,37)→(65,31).

|---|---|---|
| baron ↔ its two camps | 2.0 units (touching) | 7.2–8.25 units |

A real gap of roughly 2.5–3 cells of other terrain now sits between each camp and its pit, not

camp cells already border lane cells elsewhere on the existing hand-authored map, so this is
consistent with the map's existing style, not a new problem.

## 5. Readings

1. **The camp/pit overlap was not the cause. It's now a ruled-out hypothesis, not a fix.**
   Everything T2 and T3 flagged as "traced to the same known T4 cause" held essentially flat —
   macro win vector (51.0→50.7%), gank/sandwich/multi-man connect rates, first-tower lane split
   (25/10 → 23/11). None of it moved the way the working theory predicted. That theory is now
   spent: three phases in a row can't keep pointing at the same unconfirmed cause.
2. **Blue-side win rate got worse, not better — 57.7% → 61.3%, +3.6 points, well past the ~2.9-point
   noise floor.** This is the third phase running to push this number further from the ~50% target
   (49.7% → 55.0% → 57.7% → 61.3%), and this time the change that was supposed to be the fix moved
   it the most. Terrain symmetry is not the explanation — `tools/check.sh`'s anchor-symmetry check
   passed, and it exists specifically to catch a drifted-position asymmetry (its own doc comment
   references a past incident of exactly that). Whatever is driving this is not a map-geometry bug;
   it's something about how blue vs. red plays out mechanically that three different terrain
   changes have each nudged in the same direction.
3. **Kills/min and match length are stable** (1.11→1.10, 26.9→26.9) — T4 on its own is a
   low-blast-radius change, consistent with only touching four small terrain patches far from most
   of the map's traffic.

## 6. Recommendation

**Do not sign off M6-T as done.** The scoping report billed T4 as the milestone's sign-off phase,
and the honest read of these four phases together is that the milestone's own tracked balance
metric — blue-side win rate — has moved further from target every single time, and the working
theory for why has just been ruled out. Shipping the map as-is (T1–T4 all built, all guard-rail
clean, all individually reasoned) is fine; calling the *balance* settled is not. My instinct: this
needs a real investigation pass — instrument *why* blue wins more (which role, which phase of the
game, gold curve vs. kill curve) rather than another blind terrain change hoping to move the same
symptom. That's a different kind of work than T1–T4 (a diagnosis, not a build), and worth scoping
as its own step rather than a "T5."
