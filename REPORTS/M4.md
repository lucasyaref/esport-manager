# M4 — Viewer v1 — Report

## What changed
The M3 text scoreboard is replaced by a real **top-down match viewer**. Press Play and the deterministic sim you validated in M3 now plays back on a map: ten champions move through laning, the jungler ganks, teams group for Dragon and Baron, towers fall, and a nexus explodes — with a kill feed, live scoreboard, clock, gold bar, and playback speed controls.

The architecture holds the frozen line (GDD pillar 3, *visuals are playback*): the viewer runs the sim **once** in-process, captures its event stream + per-tick snapshots, and then only *reads* them. It never touches sim state. Because `sim/` is pure GDScript, an in-process run is byte-identical to a headless one — the same seed shows the same game every time.

Two new files, both pure presentation:
- **`game/main.gd`** — the viewer/orchestrator. Owns the playback clock, walks the event stream with a cursor, interpolates champion positions between snapshots, and drives the HUD.
- **`game/map_view.gd`** — a "dumb" renderer: each frame it's handed one fully-resolved frame dict and draws it. It knows nothing about ticks or the sim.

## The map reads like LoL
Coordinates are flipped on the Y axis on the way to the screen, so **blue sits bottom-left, red top-right, mid runs the diagonal** — the orientation anyone who's seen a LoL map will parse instantly. Top lane hugs the left edge and top; bot lane the bottom and right. The river is the anti-diagonal band through the two pits.

On the field:
- **Champions** — a team-tinted disc with a **role letter** (T/J/M/C/S), a **level badge**, the player's handle, and a bar underneath. (Placeholder art per CLAUDE.md: the character's `sprite` data field is already honored — the day a PNG is dropped at that path, it's used automatically with no code change; until then it's the shared drawn placeholder.)
- **Towers** — 3 per lane per team, drawn in team colour; they turn to rubble the instant their `tower_destroyed` event passes.
- **Pits** — Dragon (orange) and Baron (purple) glow when alive; Dragon shows a running "×N taken" count.
- **Bases/nexus**, **jungle camps**, and **wards** (fading vision dots when a support wards).

## Animations & combat, from the event stream
Every readable beat is driven by a real sim event at its exact tick:
- **Move** — interpolated glide between snapshots.
- **Fight** — a pulsing ring around everyone in the fight for its duration; a clash marker at the location.
- **Ultimate** — an expanding flare plus the ult's name floating up (e.g. "Gravemaker", "Deathbloom").
- **Die** — a red "✖ handle" pops at the death spot; the champion greys out at base with a **respawn bar** that fills back to their return, then they re-appear. (Doubles as the respawn timer.)
- **Recall** — a teal channel ring, then the snap home.
- **Objectives / towers / nexus** — banners at the pit/lane ("DRAGON ▸ CRIMSON RAVENS", "TOWER", "NEXUS DESTROYED").

### One honest call: the bar under each champion
The GDD asked for an "HP bar", but the M3 sim has **no continuous HP** — combat is resolved abstractly (weighted power rolls), players are only alive or dead. Rather than fake a health value the sim doesn't have (which would misrepresent what's actually happening), the bar shows real, snapshot-derived state: **full while alive, draining to a respawn timer on death**. It reads like a health bar in the moment that matters (someone dies) without lying about the sim. If you want literal mid-fight HP later, that's a sim change (give combat intermediate HP), not a viewer patch — flagged as a question below.

## HUD & controls
- **Top bar**: game clock, each team's kill score, and a **gold bar** (share split + who's ahead by how much).
- **Side panel**: a live **scoreboard** (level / CS / K-D-A / gold per player, both teams) and a **kill feed**.
- **Bottom bar**: play/pause, **1× / 4× / 16×**, **Skip ▸ Result**, **↻ Rewatch**, **New Match** (next seed), and a **timeline you can scrub** (drag to seek anywhere, forward or back).
- **Result overlay** on game end: winner + final KDA.
- **1× ≈ 8 real minutes per match** per CLAUDE.md; 16× watches a whole game in ~30 s.

## What to look at in-game
Open the project in Godot and press Play. Then, the actual test for this milestone:

> **Watch one game at 1× and try to narrate it out loud.** "Bot lane is shoving, Havoc is ganking mid, they're grouping for Dragon, Longshot is fed and cleaning up the fight." If you can tell that story from the dots, M4's real goal is met. If any of it reads as random wandering, that's exactly the feedback I need.

Tips: use **16×** first to see the whole shape of a game fast, then **scrub back** to a teamfight and watch it at **1×**. **New Match** cycles seeds so you can sample several games; the seed is reproducible if you want to revisit one. (Dev/QA: launching with `-- --seed=N --speed=16` jumps straight into a specific fast playback.)

## Verified
- `tools/check.sh` — RNG-discipline lint, data validation, and the determinism guarantee all still **PASS** (the viewer added no sim code).
- Full match played back headless end-to-end (to nexus + result overlay) with **zero runtime errors**.
- What I could **not** self-verify is the thing that matters most here — whether it actually *looks* readable. That needs your eyes.

## Open design questions
1. **Is it watchable?** The one question only you can answer. Does a 1× game narrate? Anything that reads as noise, or any beat you expected to see and didn't (a specific play, a clearer "who's winning" cue)?
2. **The champion bar.** Is the alive/respawn bar enough, or do you want literal shrinking HP during fights? The latter means adding HP to the combat sim (a real change, and it'd shift how fights *feel*, not just look) — worth it only if the abstract fights read as unsatisfying to watch.
3. **Now watchable: the M3 numbers.** With eyes on games, do the two M3 dials still feel right — snowball at ~66% leader-win, and the 57/43 Ravens/Wolves matchup? Both are one-value tweaks; this is the milestone to judge them by feel.
