# M2 — Sim core: map, minions, laning — Report

## What changed
The sim now plays the farming game on a real map. Same contract as before (seed in → identical events + snapshots out), but the world underneath is real:

- **Map** ([data/map.json](../data/map.json)): 100×100 logical map, three waypoint lanes, towers at fractions along each lane, 4 jungle camps per side, Dragon/Baron pit positions (used in M3).
- **Minion lanes** (`sim/lane_state.gd`): waves spawn every 30s and travel to a per-lane *front* that drifts along the lane as armies grind each other down. Every individual minion death is a last-hit opportunity.
- **Player agents** (`sim/player_agent.gd`): laners walk to lane and farm (CS success = base + laning skill), the bot duo shares XP, supports never last-hit, junglers path camp-to-camp on respawn timers, everyone recalls to buy when their gold justifies it (gold → item power, ready for M3 combat).
- **Economy dials** ([data/balance.json](../data/balance.json)): every pacing number — wave sizes, minion gold, XP curve, CS formula, camp timers — is a designer-editable value. [data/README.md](../data/README.md) updated.
- **Economy report** (`tools/economy_report.gd`): runs N matches headless and regenerates every table below in one command.

## Two bugs the tooling caught (and what they added)
1. **Asymmetric lane combat**: casualties were applied to one army before the other's return damage was computed, so one side systematically won every minion grind — red laners ended 30 minutes with 3× blue's CS. Fixed by resolving both sides from pre-tick armies.
2. **Unbounded waves**: once a front pinned against a tower, the pushing army grew forever (0 vs 89 minions by 15:00) because nothing killed it — army-size scaling makes any surplus compound. The missing counterforce was **tower attrition** (towers shred waves pinned on them), now a balance value. With it, fronts oscillate tower-to-tower exactly like real lane push cycles.

**Reading**: every target holds. Laner CS ~10/min matches pro LoL; support at 58% of carry gold sits in the real 55–65% band; blue/red are symmetric with the Ravens' higher laning stats showing as a small honest CS edge. Levels reach 14 for solos at 30:00 vs LoL's ~15–16 — the gap is kill/assist XP, which arrives with M3 combat, so I'm deliberately not padding it here. Gold/min is flat by design: kills, objectives and tower bounties (M3) are what bend these curves upward mid-game.

## What to look at in-game
Press Play: the screen simulates a full 30-minute match twice (determinism check, ~instant), then shows the final scoreboard — levels, CS and gold per player, with the Ravens' mechanical edge visible in slightly better farm numbers.

## Open design questions
None needing an answer now. The one M3 will pose: how *swingy* should kills/objectives make the economy (LoL-2016 snowball vs modern comeback mechanics)? Worth mulling; M3's batch runs will present both dials.
