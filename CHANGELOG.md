## M4.5 — Sim depth: space, health, agency — done (report: REPORTS/M4.5.md)
From the designer's M4 playtest. Root cause: map positions were decorative — the sim computed outcomes from aggregate power scores. Design truth in GDD §6.1.

Designer calls so far: sim depth before the draft screen · **full spatial combat** · lane HP trading that converts into solo kills · **boundary clamp only** for the map, jungle terrain parked · minions fixed properly as **real marching squads**, not a viewer trick · chases fixed **mechanically** (leash, tower threat, target spread) before any ability kits.

Every phase ends runnable, playtested and committed. Per-phase gate: `tools/check.sh` green, `tools/batch_run.gd --sims=200` (1000 in G), and a designer playtest at 1x — that playtest is the real gate.

- **A — Foundations — done** (`f536b24`): live HP on players (regen, fountain heal, low-HP recall, HP in snapshots); per-character combat stats (`attack_range`, `preferred_range`, `attack_speed`, `fight_role`) + validation; real HP bars in the viewer; minion dots rendered from the aggregate lane front; XP curve retuned (solos were level 12–14 at 30 min, now 17.1–17.6; jungler 16.2; bot lane 15.6).
- **B — Spatial combat engine — done** (`67ccead`): `Combat.resolve()` is gone. Every tick players perceive, decide whether to commit, pick a target, steer to their character's range, and swing; deaths happen at 0 HP. Ultimates fire on conditions. A fight detector clusters engagements so the kill feed still works. Two design rules fell out of it and are now GDD truth: reach is paid for in damage (melee hit harder), and contest an objective only if you can win it. 200-sim batch: 0–3 timeouts, 34.8 min avg, 41.8 kills, first blood 6.9 min, kills spread across all lanes and all five roles. Cost 1.9 s/sim (~32 min for a 1000-sim balance run) — acceptable, and the reason combat perception runs at 5 Hz while attacks stay at 10 Hz.
  - **Known regression, for G**: gold leader at 15 min now wins only ~50% (M3: 66%, GDD target ~65%), and match length runs ~35–37 min, at the top of the 25–35 target. Same cause for both: advantage is weakly coupled to victory because sieging and lane pressure barely exist yet — a team that is behind simply declines fights and farms. Not tunable with `power_item_divisor` (swept 10000/6000/4000, no effect). Expect C–E to restore the coupling; re-measure before touching dials.
- **C — Bounds, towers, ending chases — done**. Four things the designer watched go wrong in the second playtest (2026-07-21), each verified in code first:
  1. *Players walk off the map* — nothing clamps `pos` anywhere in `sim/`, and `Combat._retreat_pos` steers away from enemies, so a player pinned against the edge keeps going. → `SimMap.clamp_pos()` over the playable area, with every position write in `sim/` routed through it (both `PlayerAgent._move_toward` and the combat branch of `PlayerAgent.update`). A few lines; ship it first and alone.
  2. *Turrets deal no damage* — `Combat._deal_damage` is only ever called with a `PlayerAgent` attacker; towers only *receive* damage, in `Objectives._update_siege`. → towers get range + dps and LoL-style aggro: minions first, else the nearest enemy player in range, switching to whoever attacks an allied player inside the zone. A tower kill has no player killer: credit the nearest diver within a generous radius, else emit an empty killer — and guard `game/main.gd:_on_kill`, which today does `kda[killer].k += 1` and would crash on it.
  3. *Infinite chasing* — no leash, no give-up rule, no chase timer exists at all. → break off when the target has been out of attack range for `chase_patience_s`, when the chase has dragged the attacker more than `leash_radius` from where it committed, or when continuing means diving a tower it cannot afford. Reuses the `disengage_until` hysteresis from B; needs `_commit_pos` / `_last_hit_at` on `PlayerAgent`.
  4. *Teamfights are five people chasing one runner* — every ally scores targets with nearly the same formula in `Combat._select_target`. → divide an enemy's score by `1 + focus_penalty * (allies already targeting it)`, read from the previous tick's assignments, so a team splits between the frontline in its face and the carry behind it.
  - Two things fell out of making towers real. **Laners now farm from outside enemy turret range** (`SimMatch.lane_stand_pos`) — walking under a turret is a dive, a fight decision, not something farming does by accident. And a **dive has to be paid for**: you only enter the zone for a target already nearly dead or with better than a 2:1 local power advantage, which is what turns a jungler's arrival into a dive and keeps a laner who merely won the trade out.
  - **Map data fixed as a consequence**: outer towers sat at lane param 0.42/0.58, so the entire laning zone was inside turret range and laners could never reach each other once turrets could shoot. Towers moved to 0.30/0.18/0.08 (mirrored), which is roughly where LoL puts them. Lane push rate raised to match the longer distance (`front_drift` 0.0001 → 0.00018).
  - 40–60 sim batches: no agent ever outside the playable area; 0 timeouts (was 10 mid-phase), 29.3 min avg length, 20 kills, first blood 9.3 min, gold-lead conversion 60% (up from ~50% — towers and dives give an advantage somewhere to land). Tower kills ~3% of all kills, none before 5 min, all during sieges and dives.
- **D — Minions march — done**: waves stopped teleporting to the front. A squad `{team, t, melee_caster, cannon}` replaced the `_incoming` arrival queue in `sim/lane_state.gd` — squads spawn at their base end, walk at `minions.speed`, stop on contact with the leading enemy squad (or a tower they can't pass), grind it down with the existing `combat_kill_rate` / `tower_kill_rate`, and a friendly squad catching a stalled one merges into it. They never leave their lane by construction, which is the "stay in lane, no chasing" the designer asked for. `front_t` **became derived** — the midpoint of the leading blue and red squads — so `farm_t`, `try_gank` overextension, `TeamBrain._pushed_lane`, `Objectives._update_siege` and the viewer keep reading it unchanged; `front_drift` is gone, pushing is now a physical consequence of winning the wave. Lane snapshot row carries `squad_rows()`; `game/main.gd:_minion_dots` draws from real squad positions. 20-sim check: squads spawn near 0.24/0.76 and meet at ~0.50, no runaway armies (peak ~22/lane), determinism holds, viewer clean. Side effect for G: fewer fights (kills ~14, length ~24 min) because lane pressure now decides more — expected, re-tuned in G.
- **E — Lane agency — done**: a lane is a contest now, not a ping-pong equation. New `sim/laning.gd` runs each tick over the laners still farming: it assigns a **stance** (push / trade / allin / freeze / back) from wave state, relative HP/power and the enemy's exposure, and on a 2s cadence **trades HP via poke** between the laners standing in each lane. The split is the important part — poke is floored high (0.63 HP) so it can *never* on its own drop a laner below the ~61% line where an equal enemy commits; it is chip pressure that pushes the loser home. The **solo kill** needs a real edge — a level/item lead or a gank's third body — and is dealt by the combat engine, which already knows how to collapse on a low, isolated enemy. The all-in stance (step under the enemy tower for the kill) is gated on the enemy being *overextended*, low, and past level 2, so you die for greed, not for sitting at 60% behind your tower. Stance also scales wave pressure, so a shover physically moves the front and exposes itself to ganks. 20-sim probe: first blood 6.4 min (was ~1.5 when poke could tip commits), solo kills spread across every role (mid/carry lead, top/jungle/support all on the board) — the "top and mid just ping-pong, all kills bot" complaint is answered. Kill count (~12) and the last push to the 25–35 min target are the D+E structural length shift, left for G's economy retune (as planned — "M3's numbers do not survive B–D").
- **F — Intent, perception, reaction — done**: `TeamBrain` grew a **blackboard**. When the jungler commits to a gank it posts a call, and *who follows up is rolled from each laner's `macro`* — a sharp player reads the play and collapses (steps up via the E all-in stance, adding the body that turns a 1v1 into a kill), a poor one keeps farming and the gank goes solo. This is the "allied botlane reacts to the gank" the FSM never did (complaint #4), and it is macro made visible (Pillar 1) — the viewer feed now says "gank bot (2 follow)" or "(no follow-up)". A shoved-in **mid roams** to a gank call on another lane instead of pushing back and forth (complaint #5). Objective decisions are **vision-gated**: `SimMatch.vision_of` (ward or a living ally near the pit) feeds `_will_contest`, so a team that cannot see the pit plays it safer — a ward at the objective is now worth placing, and the fight/trade/give call reads the enemy through vision rather than omnisciently. 30-sim probe: ~9 gank calls/game, reactor count spread 0→1→2→3 (the macro variance), ~0.8 gank-kills/game; determinism holds.
- **G — Rebalance + behavioral metrics — done**: `tools/batch_run.gd` now reports **behavior** — kill distribution by map region, solo-kill share, kills before/after 14 min, level @30 min, fight count/duration, gank coordination — and **asserts** the bugs the designer caught by eye (no agent off the map, no squad out of its lane; both PASS). Retuned to targets over repeated batches (tower/nexus HP up for length, lane-commit margin up for first blood, softer gold→power + shutdowns + fight variance for snowball, softer mechanics for team balance). 120-sim sign-off: side 48/52 ✓, length 26.3 min ✓ (117/120 in 20–35), first blood 4.6 min, 18.6 kills, 22% before 14 min, 23.6 fights/match, kills spread across all regions and roles. **Two soft misses left as designer calls** (in the report): gold-lead conversion 70.8% (target ~65%) and the Wolves/Ravens split 30/70 (M3 was 43/57) — same mechanics-vs-macro tension, whose clean fix is a macro win-vector (a feature, not a dial).

## M5 history — phases A–D (done)
Context: M5 is "macro play: cross-map coordination" — see BACKLOG.md for the milestone's live status and remaining phases. This section is the record of how phases A–D got there.

**Decisions (locked 2026-07-23, from designer):** (a) **M5 before M6** — the draft screen waits. (b) **Blend gating** — a deterministic baseline of coordination always fires (every team reads as a team on screen), and each roster's `macro` scales the *quality and frequency* on top; macro stays visible per Pillar 1 and this is the lever on the 30/70 split. (c) **Lane swap as a team state**, not free reassignment: `TeamBrain` holds a `lane_assignment` formation (a role→lane map); entering a swap mutates members' `lane` — already a mutable field distinct from `role` ([player_agent.gd:21](sim/player_agent.gd#L21)), so positioning/farming follow for free. The enemy detects a non-standard assignment and can **mirror/counter** it. Ship `standard` + `bot_top_swap` + the mirror rule first; architect the map generally so **mid-collapse and 1-3-1** (start bot+top, then go mid) drop in later with no rework. (Note: the opening-timing model in (c) was later found wrong by the designer and reframed in M5-D — see below.)

- **M5-A ✅ done (f037352) — Macro intent layer + blend gating.** `TeamBrain.macro_gate` primitive; `data/balance.json` `macro` block; GDD §6.1 macro model; first-tower batch metric. Bit-identical, behaviour-neutral foundation.
- **M5-B ✅ done (f0f8794) — Lane swap as state + first-tower objective (item 4).** `lane_assignment` formation (`standard`/`bot_top_swap`), macro-gated swap + enemy mirror, first-tower gold swing. Result: gold-lead conversion 70.8% → **63.2%** (macro converts through **tower/objective currency**); first-tower-is-bot 13% → 26%.

**M5-C diagnostic (2026-07-24) — roams tried and reverted.** A first cut of laner roams (items 2 & 5) was built, measured, and **reverted**: it made the *macro* team (azure) lose *more* (46.7% → 33.3%). Cause — macro-gated roams generate proactive fight-plays, and with **equal move speed and no CC** those plays whiff, costing lane presence for no payoff; the macro team roams most, so it eats the most whiffs (confirmed: azure roamed 113 vs crimson 95, and the side that roams more loses). This is the root of the 30/70 split: macro converts through towers/objectives (M5-B worked) but not through fights (whiffed). Constraint found: the only signature spell was the **ultimate**, unlocking at **level 6** ([player_agent.gd:181](sim/player_agent.gd#L181)), so CC could not catch *early-laning* targets.

**Three-action model (locked 2026-07-24, from designer).** Resolves that constraint. Every character now has **three actions**: auto-attack, one **basic ability** (active spell *or* passive, unlocks **early**), and the **ultimate** (level 6). CC moves onto the **basic ability** for a *few* characters (jungle/support skew), so a CC ganker catches a target *in lane*, not only after 6. Rationale (reviewed for future complexity, kept): the basic ability reuses the ult's exact data shape and firing rule — a second instance of an existing machine, not a new subsystem; passives are the default and near-free in sim, active basic spells only where the kit wants them, so the count of AI-driven abilities stays low. GDD §3 + §6.1 updated. Plays read as **tempo trades** (a connecting gank buys a turret; the enemy earns tempo across the map).

- **M5-C ✅ done — Three-action model + CC that catches.** Basic-ability slot on every character (same shape as the ult; `_try_ultimate` generalized to `_try_ability` firing either slot on its own cooldown/unlock). Four CC carriers (Fenrik/Thornmaw jungle, Gromm/Vessia support) get an early **slow** basic that catches; the other 11 take a cheap passive (power/bulwark/sustain, persistent modifiers). Ganks gated on **connectability** (CC carrier on hand, or target low/overextended) so whiffs don't launch. Key mechanic learned: a slow only catches if **held until the target is at catch range** (`fight.cc_catch_range`) — fired at max range it whiffs (~2% → 15% connect). Result (200 sims): **macro team recovered 30% → 40%** (toward 43/57), gold-lead conversion 63.2% → **59.4%** (softer snowball), jungle kill-share 7% → 10%, first-tower-is-bot 26% → 44%, assertions PASS. Report: `REPORTS/M5-C.md`. Two flags carried to M5-D: **blue-side win rate crept to 56%** (catch tuning), and Azure at 40 not 43 (the coordination half is M5-D/E). Corvyn's `aoe_cc` ult now applies a real slow (correctness fix from the unified CC path; non-signature).

**Designer 1x playtest (2026-07-25), after M5-C.** Six observations. Two are a **readability wall**: fights and CC are not legible enough to judge the sim underneath them (remark 4 — "I did not see the control… just not visible"). Since the 1x playtest is the real gate, sim work the designer can't see can't be gated — so a **viewer/readability milestone (M5.5)** was inserted before the draft (M6) and recommended *before* the deeper macro phases. Three are sim-behaviour corrections folded into M5-D. One reshapes the lane-swap concept.

1. *Characters overlap / stack on one point* — kills readability. No separation exists in `sim/` (pure steering) or the viewer (raw-position dots). → **M5.5** (light deterministic separation).
2. *Fights are unreadable — spells, auto-attacks, turret HP and ultimate impact aren't shown.* The viewer draws movement + text popups but no combat juice. Designer: **critical before the draft screen.** → **M5.5**, the core of it.
3. *Botlane always ends up top; the swap concept is wrong.* Confirmed: `bot_top_swap` is an **opening** swap fired at t=0 and mirrored by the enemy ([team_brain.gd:88](sim/team_brain.gd#L88); `swap_base` 0.35 + `mirror_base` 0.6), so both bot duos teleport top before minions, decoupled from any tower. The designer's model is **objective-triggered**: bot duo starts bot, focuses bot with the jungler to take **bot T1**, and only *then* rotates — to top if top T1 still stands, else mid. → reframed item 4; built in **M5-D**.
4. *The CC / "control" was not visible* — the `basic_cast` popup exists but doesn't read as a catch. A visibility gap, not (necessarily) a sim one. → **M5.5**, and the reason M5.5 comes before more macro.
5. *Jungler yo-yos: clears bot-side, ganks top, then walks all the way back to bot-side while top-side camps are up.* Confirmed bug: `_pick_camp` keeps a stale `_camp_index` target after a gank instead of re-picking the nearest camp from where it now stands ([player_agent.gd:439](sim/player_agent.gd#L439)). → **M5-D**, cheap fix.
6. *Late game: nobody defends the base, and an over-extended pusher isn't punished.* `defend` needs the front pinned exactly on the tower line (`_pushed_lane`, [team_brain.gd:206](sim/team_brain.gd#L206)) so it rarely fires, and there is **no** "collapse on an isolated deep enemy" logic at all. → **M5-D** (late-game macro).

- **M5-D ✅ done — Behavioural corrections from the playtest** (report: `REPORTS/M5-D.md`). (i) **jungle re-pick** — `gank_over()` drops the stale camp target so the jungler clears the nearest camp after a gank instead of yo-yoing back (remark 5). (ii) **lane-swap reframed to objective-triggered** — no more opening swap; a team takes the enemy's **bot outer** then rotates the bot duo top→(else mid, new `bot_mid_swap` formation), enemy reads and matches (remark 3, item 4 redux). (iii) **base defense** — a deep home tower (inner/base/nexus) under real pressure now outranks siege/objective (remark 6's "protect the base"); the **punish-over-extension** collapse is built but **gated off** (dragged the macro team as a blunt team-wide yank — enabled properly in M5-F's multi-man layer). A **jungle bot-focus** gank-bias was tried and gated off (backfired: starved solo lanes). 200-sim result: **blue-side 56%→49.5%** (M5-C flag fixed), **gold-conversion 59.4%→66.3%** (on target), **Azure 40%→36.5%** (opening-swap prop removed — earned back in E/F), length 26.9, assertions PASS, determinism holds.

## M5-F1 — The answer, the defended base, the kill audit — done (report: REPORTS/M5-F1.md)
From the designer's 2026-07-26 playtest, second note (six observations, after M5-E shipped the sandwich).

**The audit came first, and it reframed the brief.** The batch runner gained a **kills-per-minute timeline** (five-minute bands, each divided by the minutes actually *played* in that band across the batch — a raw count makes late bands look empty because most matches have ended), **deaths by role**, and a **nexus-defence probe** (every 2 s a nexus takes damage, the sim reports how many defenders are standing on it). Grounded against LCK 2025 (222 games, 28 kills / 32:10) and LEC 2025 Spring (106 games, 28 kills / 33:42) — both ~**0.85 combined kills per minute**. The sim measured **0.84**, with a pro-like shape over the clock (~6 kills by 15 min). So "not enough deaths" was true but mis-attributed: the kill *rate* was already right; **matches were five minutes shorter than pro games** (27.1 vs 32), and **deaths were concentrated on mid + carry** (3.95 / 3.4 per team-game against support 1.4, top 1.3 — pro is close to flat, with support usually highest).

- **The answer (remark 1)** — *"the blue jungler passes behind red mid while his own T1 is being attacked and goes to a jungle camp; does not look like human-pro decision"*. Root cause was structural: every play the sim could make was one the jungler **chose** (gank, sandwich), rolled on a 15 s cadence. A third trigger, the **answer**, runs on a 2 s clock with no phase gate, in two shapes — a team-mate in a fight we are *behind* in (outnumbered, or nearly dead: an even 1v1 trade is deliberately not an answer), or an enemy on a tower of ours that is **genuinely under siege** (their wave pinned on our tower line, not just a laner standing near a turret; at our own tower the bar is lower, because an even 1v1 there is a free 2v1). Walks *at* the spot, no cut-off — the target is already committed. Posts to the blackboard so the lane collapses with him; feed says "answers". **Deliberately not `macro`-gated**: answering at your own turret is baseline competence, not a macro edge, and the gated version punished the macro roster for the same reason roams failed twice. Tuning took four passes (33 calls/match → 22 → 15 → **3.5**). Priced over 400 sims: **+4 pts gank connect, −4 pts macro win**.
- **Base defence (remark 5)** — the probe showed **2.39 defenders present** and only 15% of samples with nobody home, so they *were* coming back; they simply could not act. Two bugs: lane presence was a **lane assignment** (only a FARMING player whose `lane` matched counted, so a defender in GROUPING did not exist to the minion sim), and players could only kill minions where **two waves had collided** (so a wave walking at an exposed nexus was invulnerable). Both fixed — presence is positional, and a wave pinned on your own tower line with no counter-wave can be killed. Plus the designer's "at least one player, not necessarily all the team": the defend dispatch is sized to the threat (`defense.defend_men` = 3 for an outer tower, everyone for a base/nexus). Measured cost of the dispatch change: **none**.
- **Turret plating (remark 6)** — first tower fell at **5.6 min** against pro's ~10–12, ending laning before it produced anything. Towers now take `plating_reduction` (55%) less damage at minute zero, easing to none by minute 14. The only change in the phase that *helped* the macro roster (**+3 pts**), and the lever behind first tower **5.6 → 7.5 min**, length **27.1 → 29.0**, kills **22.9 → 26.3**.
- **Support survivability (remark 3) — built, measured, rejected.** Hypothesis was target selection (threat is built from damage and gold; the support has least of both, so nobody ever targeted it). Added killability weighting with gold counting toward durability (`fight.squishy_bias`). Support share of deaths: 12.3% off → 12.4% at 2.0 → 13.0% at 4.0. **Gated off** (wired, like M5-D's collapse). The diagnosis the numbers point at: the support does not survive fights, it is **not in them** — 43% of kills happen in mid lane, 12% in top. Ruled out: the healing (Lumen's shield is ~77 HP at level 6, ~7% of a bar). → M5-G.

**A near-miss worth recording.** The first cut gave a player at a lane front full wave-clearing weight everywhere. It broke the game — bot towers at **3.5 min**, **93%** of first towers bot, the 15-min gold leader winning **93%**, macro roster at **3.3%**. Ablations (one 200-sim run per arm) traced it to the lane-presence magnitude, not the answer. Attempts to use that magnitude as a balance dial failed: the win split responds **non-monotonically** (open-lane discount 1.0 → 34%, 0.5 → 30.5%, 0.25 → 52%), because lane equilibria are threshold-y. Shipped the setting that perturbs lane pushing least, and put the fidelity-vs-balance trade to the designer with the table rather than tuning a knob that isn't one.

**200 sims, seeds 5000–5199** (second independent set at 7000–7199 in brackets): macro win 36.0% → **34.5%** (33.0%) — inside noise; blue-side **49.5%** (51.0%); length 27.1 → **29.0** (29.2); kills 22.9 → **26.3** (26.1); kills/min 0.84 → **0.91** (0.89); first tower 5.6 → **7.5** (7.4); gank connect 17% → **22%** (23%); tempo windows 0.65 → **1.18** (1.08); sandwiches 6.12 → **4.96** (answers share the jungler's commitment clock); timeouts **0**; assertions **PASS**; determinism holds. Flag for M5-G: gold-lead conversion read 67.3% on one seed set and 73.5% on the other — a wider spread than is comfortable, worth a bigger batch during the balance pass.

## M5-F2 — Coordinated multi-man plays, and the gank set-up that failed — done (report: REPORTS/M5-F2.md)
The designer's last two coordination items (1 and 3). One shipped as the largest single gain in M5; the other was a design of the designer's own that the numbers contradicted, so it was reverted and put back to them as a question rather than quietly dropped.

- **The committed play (item 3) — shipped, +4 pts.** The detector it fires on has existed since M5-D: an enemy caught over-extended and *alone* on our half. What never worked was the **answer** to it — `intent = "collapse"`, a team-wide yank pulling all five off their lanes, which lost the macro roster points (it rolls the gate more often, so it over-commits more often) and had been gated to zero ever since. The fix was not a better detector but a **committed set**: a target, the bodies that *agreed* to come, and a 12 s window, with everyone else carrying on farming or sieging. Three properties carry it — joining is rolled **per player against that player's own `macro`** (nearest asked first), a play below `play_min_men` (2) is **never called** at all (one body walking at an over-extended enemy is the 1v1 whiff that sank both roam attempts, and it costs the farm either way), and the set **stays committed** for the window: the team's own intent cannot walk those players off mid-collapse, and the play never interrupts a recall, camp clear or gank already in flight. `TeamBrain._consider_play` + `PlayerAgent.set_play`; `defense.punish_base`/`punish_lift` off zero for the first time plus seven `defense.play_*` tunables. **Settles designer items 2 and 5 by construction** — the support roams as part of a committed play, never on its own dice — with no separate roam mechanic built.
- **Why it beats the rule.** M5-F1 established that *anything raising the number of fights favours the mechanical roster* (Crimson wins straight fights; Azure wins through towers and time). The committed play **breaks it**: kills per match 26.3 → 27.3 *and* +4 pts to the macro roster. A coordinated collapse is not a straight fight — it is a fight whose numbers edge was decided before it started, which is exactly what macro is meant to buy.
- **The gank set-up (item 1) — built, measured at −7.5 pts, reverted.** GDD §6.1 had said (from the 2026-07-26 direction) that the laner *holds* the target while the play is inbound and commits when the jungler lands. Built as a two-stage call (`setup` → `commit`, a new lane stance). It also had a genuine bug on the first pass — `setup` fell through `lane_stand_pos`'s tower-safety pullback, so a laner "setting up" walked *backwards* out of tower range, the exact opposite of the intent — and **fixing that did not rescue it**: 27.0% macro win against a 34.5% baseline, gank connect 22% → 17%, snowball worsening to 71.5%. The reason is this sim's physics, not tuning: **engaging early is what pins the victim**. A laner that merely holds leaves the victim free to disengage the moment a third body appears, so the gank arrives at a target already leaving. Same principle the answer already encodes ("walk *at* the spot, not past it — the target is already committed"), and the mirror of why the sandwich's cut-off *does* work (there the victim is deep and the geometry denies the retreat). Reactors all-in on hearing the call, as before; the **telegraph half of item 1 already existed** (the call posts when the jungler commits, not on arrival). GDD §6.1 rewritten to match what is true.
- **Method note.** Both mechanics were built before either was measured, and the combined first run looked like a flat failure (30.5% macro win). Only ablation separated them — committed play alone **38.5%**, set-up alone **27.0%**, both **30.5%**, against a 34.5% baseline: close to additive, and the shipped half would have been thrown away with the other if the phase had stopped at the combined number.
- **200 sims, seeds 5000–5199** (second independent set 7000–7199 in brackets): macro win 34.5% → **38.5%** (36.0%); blue-side **49.5%** (49.0%); gold-lead conversion 67.3% → **66.5%** (64.5%) — the uncomfortable 67.3/73.5 spread M5-F1 flagged has closed and both sit on the ~65% target; length 29.0 → **29.5** (29.5); kills 26.3 → **27.3** (26.6); first tower **7.6** (7.6); gank connect **21%** (22%); plays **7.83/match** (7.49) at **2.25 men** (2.28) and **24% connect** (23%); timeouts **0**; assertions **PASS**; determinism holds.

## M5-G — Balance pass, batch sign-off, and the fight-size diagnosis — done (report: REPORTS/M5.md)
The phase that was scoped as "re-measure and tune" turned into a diagnosis, because M6-A's scorer (shipped alongside, see below) made a question answerable that had never been answerable before. **M5 signs off with the macro win-vector target met: 42.5% against ~43%**, from 34.5% before M5-F2.

- **The instrument first.** Building the highlight scorer forced `peak` and `present` onto `fight_end`, and with them the sim could be asked how big fights get *and why*. The M6 scoping report's "no fight has ever reached six participants" pre-dated M5-E/F and is no longer true — the average match's biggest fight is 5.7 bodies. But 65% of fights are two bodies, and the reason turned out not to be macro at all: **at the average fight 3.9 bodies stand within reach of an enemy and only 2.6 swing.** The teams converge; the missing bodies are present and declining. So `fight_end` also carries *why* each declined, and the phase went after the reasons instead of adding more plays.
- **The reinforcement was locked out of the fight it walked into — fixed.** 69% of "standing there and not swinging" was the disengage lock. That lock is hysteresis for *breaking off*, but it was applied on **every** decline, including "I have not arrived yet": a third man walking into a 1v1 sees the two enemies before his own ally enters his awareness radius, reads the local numbers as 1v2, declines, and is barred from the fight for twelve seconds — twice the average fight's length. Now it only applies to a body that was actually in the fight. Seeds 5000–5199: kills 27.3 → **31.4**, fights per match 26.2 → **33.6**, moments where a side loses 2+ 2.21 → **2.77**.
- **The death clock — the one balance change that shipped.** `combat.respawn_per_level_s` 1.4 → **2.2**, so a level-18 death costs 47.6 s instead of 33 (real LoL is ~50 s). One number, four targets the right way: macro win 37.5% → **39.5%**, kills 31.4 → **28.4** (pro: 28), gold-lead conversion 68.0% → **64.0%** (target ~65%), longest match in the batch 40.9 → **38.8 min** — which is as close as the phase got to the inherited *decisive endgame* item. 3.0 is worse on every count, so 2.2 is a peak and not a slope.
- **Four things measured and rejected**, each at 200 sims. (1) *Shortening the disengage lock further*: 12 → 6 → 3 s takes kills to 35.7 then 39.5 against pro's 28, while the two-body share does not move at all (66/64/64%) — the dial buys kills, not fights. (2) *Lowering `fight.damage_scale`* 2.2 → 1.9 → 1.7 → 1.5 to lengthen fights: duration stayed at 6 s in every arm, because that 6 s is mostly the 5 s end-grace tail and real contact is one to three seconds; match length went *down*. (3) *Scaling siege damage by the numbers* instead of the on/off switch where two defenders zero out five attackers — more faithful, and a real explanation for the bleeding nexus, but the nexus probe moved only 28.8 → 27.2 samples and the longest match 40.9 → 39.9 while the macro roster paid 3.5 pts and blue-side drifted to 55%; reverted. (4) *Holding a won fight at low HP* (`fight.hold_when_winning_edge`) — built, works mechanically (low-HP declines 19% → 7%, multi-death moments 2.42 → 3.03, biggest fight 5.8 → 6.1) but the two-body share barely moves and it costs kill fidelity and 2.5 pts; **shipped gated off at 99** as a designer call, the same treatment `fight.squishy_bias` got.
- **What could not be fixed, and why it matters.** The share of fights that are two bodies sat at 63–66% across *sixteen* measured arms. Bodies arrive, exchange for one to three seconds, break off, and the next exchange scores as a new fight — which is why fixing the lockout took fights per match from 26 to 34 while leaving fight *size* alone. Making a real 5v5 happen is a change to how long a committed body stays committed: a change to the combat model in GDD §6.1, not to a number in it. Recorded there with a ⚠️, and it is the prerequisite M6's close-up view is waiting on.
- **Sign-off, 200 sims per cell on seeds 5000–5199 and 7000–7199.** Macro roster win **39.5% / 45.5% → 42.5%** (target ~43%); blue-side **51.5% / 49.5%**; gold-lead conversion **64.0% / 57.3%** (target ~65%, and the seed-set spread is wider than M5-F2's); kills **28.4 / 28.0** (pro: 28); length **29.2 / 29.0** (pro: 32); CKPM **0.97 / 0.97** (pro: ~0.85); first tower **7.4 / 7.4** (pro: 10–12); reel moments **8.1 / 7.9** (target 5–10); timeouts **0**; assertions **PASS**; determinism holds.

## M6-A — Highlight scoring, headless (report: REPORTS/M6-A.md)
Pulled forward to ship alongside M5-G, as the backlog planned: the selection layer is cheap, pure and headless-testable, and it doubles as the balance instrument M5-G turned out to need.

- **`sim/highlights.gd`** reads a finished match's event stream and returns the moments worth watching. Pure analysis — it never runs inside the sim and cannot influence it. Kills, fights, objectives and structures all become **anchors** with a time span and a position; anchors overlapping in *both* are one moment. That single rule is why a gank, the tower it buys and the dragon that follows come out as one moment rather than three, with no special-casing. Score = kills + multikills + ace + bodies at the peak + gold swing + objective/structure value, all multiplied by game clock (`late_bonus`). Selection applies the designer's absolute floor from GDD §7.2 (a quiet game gets a *short* reel, never a padded one), spacing, a per-kind cap and a hard count; the nexus is exempt from all four. Deterministic by construction — one ordered pass, every sort tie broken by the event's own index. Tunables in `data/highlights.json`, none of which can change how a match plays.
- **Event-stream support.** `fight_end` gained the participant lists, the gold swing, and `peak` — the largest either side ever got *at once*, because a member list reaches five over a 20 s scrap without four bodies ever standing together. `kill` gained `victim_team`; `objective_taken` / `tower_destroyed` / `nexus_destroyed` gained `pos` so moments can merge spatially. `SimMap.region()` moved up out of `batch_run` so the reports and the reel name places identically (same constants, no reported number changed).
- **`tools/reel.gd`** prints one match's reel, `--all` for every candidate and its score — that is the designer gate. `batch_run` reports moments per match, reel score, kind mix, spread over the clock, the biggest fight of the match and the full fight-size distribution.
- **First reading, 200 sims:** 7.8 moments per reel (inside the designer's 5–10 with no tuning of the floor), 47 candidates, 2.2 moments where a side loses 2+, and a reel spread of **2% / 31% / 67%** early/mid/late — the early game contains nothing worth watching, so GDD §7.2's reserved laning slot would today be reserving a slot for nothing.

## M5.5 — Viewer v2: combat readability & juice — done (report: REPORTS/M5.5.md)
Inserted between M5-D and M5-E after the 2026-07-25 playtest: the sim's combat depth (M4.5) and its CC (M5-C) were invisible, and since the designer's 1x playtest is the real gate, sim work they cannot see cannot be gated. Sim stays the source of truth (Pillar 3) — everything here is playback, with two deliberate, measured exceptions (phase A's rejected separation, phase H's wave doorstep).

- **A — Champions stop stacking.** Shipped as a **playback layout pass** (`MatchViewer._spread_bodies`): overlapping bodies are relaxed apart for drawing, splitting each overlap evenly so the group's centre of mass — "the dot is where the fight is" — survives. The **sim-side** version was built and measured first and **not shipped**: same 200 seeds, giving bodies volume cost the macro team 6 points (36.5% → 30.5%), undid M5-D's blue-side fix (49.5% → 43.5%) and pushed gold-lead conversion to 71.1%, while *raising* gank connect (16% → 22%) and first-tower-is-bot (10% → 27%). Bodies having volume is a balance decision, so it went to M5-E/F as a tunable lever, not a rendering fix. The designer accepted the drawn version (2026-07-25) and confirmed the macro cost is tunable later.
- **B — The attack beat + the catch.** Snapshot player rows gained three report-only columns (target row, combat flags in/disengaging/stunned/slowed/recalling, tick of the last swing) plus a `cc_applied` event. The viewer draws every swing the sim fires (projectile for ranged, slash for melee, spark on landing) and CC as a **catch**: a mark on the victim for exactly the sim's lock duration, and a dashed tether back to the caster. Answers remark 4, "I did not see the control".
- **C — Ultimate impact.** `ultimate_cast`/`basic_cast` carry position, radius and targets hit; one shape per effect family (shockwave at the real radius, beam / piercing snipe / execute cross, heal-shield bloom, self-buff aura), always louder from the ult slot than the basic slot.
- **D — Structure HP.** Chipped towers and nexus HP ride on the snapshot; the viewer draws them, so a siege reads as sustained pressure and a dive under a low turret reads as a race.
- **E — Placeholder combat sprites.** Procedural per-role silhouettes (hexagon top, arrowhead jungle, star mid, pentagon carry, octagon-cross support), team-tinted, turned toward target or path. The data-driven `sprite` field still overrides them with real art and no code change (CLAUDE.md).
- **F — Report + proof of report-only.** A 30-seed fingerprint (winner, length, kills and gold per side) matched the pre-M5.5 commit exactly and the 200-sim baseline reproduced M5-D's numbers to the decimal, so B–E's sim additions are provably observational. `tools/check.sh` gained a **headless viewer selftest**: it plays a whole match through the real playback code with no display and fails if the visuals the sim's events imply were never drawn.
- **G — Playtest corrections (2026-07-25 notes).** Three of the designer's four notes had one root cause: **1x playback runs 4× sim-time** (40 ticks a real second against the sim's 10), and every visual lifetime was written in *sim* ticks — so an ult impact lived 0.65 s and an attack beat 0.12 s. They were glimpses because they *were* glimpses. Lifetimes are now sized in real seconds at 1x and stretch with the speed button, capped at 4x. Also: the amber "fighting" halo meant `in_combat`, which the sim sets on standoffs across a wave too (measured: only **30%** of in-combat player-frames are real exchanges) — it now means *trading blows*, in hot red so it stops competing with the amber CC mark, and disengaging draws retreat chevrons; HP deltas between snapshots become **floating damage numbers** and a hit-flash (the bars *were* moving — 2 px a swing on a 22 px bar); turrets drain like a battery, leave rubble, and pulse orange while losing HP, with turrets-standing and both nexus healths permanently in the side panel and feed lines for every turret, an exposed nexus and each nexus threshold; the ult caster flares white and gets a feed line with its body count. `--selftest` asserts all of it and prints the closing feed lines, so "can this ending be narrated?" is checked on every run.
- **H — The wave that takes the nexus is visible (2026-07-26 notes).** The designer, watching phase G, correctly diagnosed the silent ending: "in reality minions are hitting nexus". Two halves. *Viewer:* a squad chipping a structure right now is drawn swinging at it — each minion pokes on its own beat, matched by proximity to a structure that is actually losing HP, so nothing is drawn that the sim is not doing. *Sim (the deliberate exception):* an open lane's push bound was `0.06`/`0.94`, which is **11–14 world units from the nexus, an eighth of the map** — the wave really did drain the nexus from a screen-distance away, so the designer's "no enemy hitting nexus, too far from it" was literal truth, not a rendering gap. The bound is now `towers.open_lane_front` in `data/balance.json` (0.015, the base doorstep), tunable because it is also how fast a won lane converts. The selftest now fails if a structure is chipped with no besieging minion drawn, or if a nexus falls with nothing drawn hitting it — the exact bug the designer caught, now caught headlessly. Measured over the same 200 seeds M5-D signed off on: blue-side 49.5% → 47.0%, Azure (macro) 36.5% → **33.0%**, gold-lead conversion 66.3% → **64.8%** (onto target), length 26.9 → 27.1 min, assertions PASS — each win-rate move is about one standard error at n=200, so a nudge rather than a regression. Notably the **pacing did not change**: the wave reaches the nexus now, but still bleeds it slowly, so M5-G keeps the decisive-endgame item (with `open_lane_front` as a new dial).

## Retired — the sim-macro critic pipeline (2026-08-08)

`AGENT_HARNESS.md` and its two subagents (`lol-macro-critic`, `tuning-analyst`) are deleted. They
specified a review pipeline for **sim macro fidelity**: a critic grounding the project's own targets
(43/57 macro split, ~65% gold-lead conversion, first-tower timing) against real pro-play numbers via
web search, and an analyst turning its findings into `TUNING_BACKLOG.md` entries.

Recorded here because the files were never tracked in git, so this is the only surviving account.

Why retired: the pipeline was half-built and unused. Part A — a structured `REPORTS/batch_metrics.json`
export from `tools/batch_run.gd` — was specified as the critic's preferred input and never written, so
the critic could only ever work from prose tables. Part C never produced a single entry; `TUNING_BACKLOG.md`
does not exist. Meanwhile M5 signed off (`REPORTS/M5.md`) with its macro win-vector target met at 42.5%
against ~43%, reached by ordinary batch measurement without the pipeline.

The idea it was right about — that a critic must be **grounded in something outside the thing being
judged**, and must emit findings in a fixed format with a severity and a category that decides who acts
on them — is kept, and is the shape of the map gauntlet's critics (`docs/gauntlet-map.md`). If sim-macro
review is ever wanted again, build Part A first; a critic reading prose is a critic reading our own
opinion of the numbers.
